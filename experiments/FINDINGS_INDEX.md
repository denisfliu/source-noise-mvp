## Pin construction & pass-through mechanism
- **The pin clamps a chosen orthonormal source-subspace coordinate `c=Uᵀa`; the flow generates the complement conditioned on it — training pins `c` to the action's own coord so FM velocity is 0 on `U`, the coord is never denoised (pass-through=identity).** — ARC §1; MEM:284-305 (`steer_passthrough.py`, `results/passthrough.json`).
- **Pass-through holds for ANY orthonormal `U` (preserves linear flow path + isotropic Gaussian source); non-orthonormal/nonlinear (OAT latent) bottleneck breaks it.** — ARC §1,§3; MEM:479-490.
- **Pass-through binds exactly at D=192 (6-DOF): realized-minus-commanded coord err ~0.01-0.04 vs |cmd|~13 (0.1-0.3%).** — ARC §2.4; MEM:521.
- **Instrumented ODE: pinned bin-1 coeff of `x_t` is dead-constant = command across all 20 Euler steps, velocity along pin = exactly 0; conditioning instead generates the coeff (0.25→1.94).** — MEM:284-298 (`results/passthrough.json`).
- **Pin's slope-1 exactness + 2× extrapolation are tautological (reads back the clamp); the non-trivial content is the complement being denoised around the clamp.** — MEM:294-305.
- **Hard pin = maximal variance collapse on `U` by construction; benign iff target conditional variance on `U` is low, else it underfits (the obedience tax).** — MEM:1010-1030.
- **Pin implementation (exact, matches pi0): `noise = (I-UUᵀ)g + Uc`; `x_t=t·noise+(1-t)a`; `u_t=noise-a`.** — `rung3/pca_pin.py:build_pca_noise` (MEM:1396-1397).

## Basis choice (Fourier / DCT / grid-Laplacian / PCA / RRR / learned-U)
- **For a single body with smooth structure, learning `U` (generalized eigvecs of Sb/Sw) is within noise of fixed Fourier and never beats it; learned-basis direction retired.** — ARC §2.3; MEM:479-511 (`toy_embodiment/learned_basis_toy.py`).
- **Basis family matters only under channel coupling or embodiment change: with 6-DOF pos-ori coupling, cross-channel grid-Laplacian (GLAP) >> per-channel Fourier≈scratch; without coupling per-channel Fourier is best and GLAP has no advantage.** — ARC §2.5 (`pose6d_hard_c1/c0_result.json`); MEM:544-564.
- **Decisive causal control: coupling-weight sweep `w=0→2` — pin at/below scratch at w=0 (no coupling), above scratch for w>0.** — ARC §2.5 (`glap_sweep_result.json`); MEM:531-533.
- **Grid-Laplacian is the DOF-agnostic general basis (defined by a graph over time×DOF; specified for any channel count without relearning).** — MEM:622-623 (`laplacian_basis.py`).
- **For cross-embodiment TRANSFER, invariance (not coverage) drives success: GLAP transfer 0.75 >> PCA 0.34 on held-out Jaco despite PCA reconstructing better; success tracks c-body-invariance.** — `rung3/transfer6d_pca_result.json`; MEM:938-970.
- **PCA "completeness" is actively harmful to the pin: its top-K absorb body-specific low-variance coords → prior can't predict them → wrong command; GLAP's incompleteness is correct.** — MEM:948-970.
- **Coherence generalized-eigenbasis (max Sb/Sw) FAILS as a pin basis (~0.75 rel err ≈ random): pure invariance ignores energy → ~zero coverage.** — MEM:919-930.
- **On LIBERO actions grid-Laplacian is a POOR instruction basis: top-16 GLAP capture only 0.197 of action variance (LIBERO actions are contact-rich/bang-bang, not smooth-low-rank).** — `rung3/vc_precheck.py`; MEM:1032-1051.
- **Data-fit PCA is the right VLA basis: PCA-16 coverage 0.94-0.956, cross-task rel err 0.24 vs grid-Lap 0.87 (~random).** — `openpi/make_u_pca.py`, `pin_U_pca.npy`; MEM:1053-1058.
- **RRR (action subspace predictable from state+language) beats PCA in every multi-task LIBERO regime keeping PCA-like coverage; V_useful goal PCA 32.6 / RRR 37.0 / LDA 0.9.** — `rung3/analyze_bases.py`; MEM:1407-1426 (`pin_U_rrr_k5_shared.npy`).
- **LDA is useless as a pin basis: high between-instruction ratio but coverage ~0.3-1.4%.** — `rung3/build_u_lang.py`; MEM:1407-1418.
- **For the gate (single-scene + L/R), RRR ≈ PCA on held c-R² (0.966 vs 0.959): PCA's max-variance dirs already ARE the predictable ones; RRR only wins when high-variance content is NOT obs-predictable.** — `rung3/make_u_rrr_gate.py`; MEM:1920-1927.
- **RRR flow WINS BIG closed-loop where the c-R² near-tie did NOT predict it: RRR flow + progress prior does the demo turn 100% / in-zone 80% vs PCA flow turn 10% / 60%.** — MEM:2011-2020.

## Instruction encoding (one-hot / text-embed / VLM grounding)
- **Keyword one-hot is robust in-domain but collapses on unseen tasks (state+onehot subspace-R² -1.9 on held-out LIBERO) — no learned column to generalize.** — `rung3/vlm_rrr_libero.py`; MEM:1578-1582.
- **Frozen sentence-embedding (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) WASHES OUT the left/right minimal pair: cos(orig-L,orig-R)=0.998 (between-gate 0.002) but within-gate paraphrase dist 0.15-0.27 → phrasing ~100× the L/R signal; not fixable by augmentation.** — `rung3/gate_text_embed.py`; MEM:1532-1544.
- **Text embeddings pay off only when instructions vary SEMANTICALLY (objects/goals), not for minimal-pair spatial contrasts (left/right).** — MEM:1541-1544.
- **VLM grounding is the general fix: define `U` as the VLM-predictable action subspace (RRR on pi0 PaliGemma feats) + VLM→c prior; generalizes to unseen tasks (context +0.40) where one-hot collapses (-1.9).** — `rung3/vlm_rrr_libero.py`; MEM:1566-1585.
- **Fusion helps OOD: contextualized (post-LLM) VLM feature > pre-fusion prefix on unseen language goals (-0.17→+0.06); in-distribution they tie.** — MEM:1578-1582.

- **True (ridge-conditioned) VLM-RRR c goes THROUGH the right gate where scratch, state+onehot-RRR, and ill-conditioned VLM-c all missed — first to route through the aperture.** — MEM:2079-2100 (`rung3/serve_gate_pin_vlmc.py`, `rung3/vlmc_ridge_prefusion.npz`).

## Prior / coordinate prediction (obs→c)
- **The prior is THE bottleneck/ceiling everywhere (oracle >> prior across toy/robosuite/LIBERO); pin+prior transfer is sound, basis is secondary.** — ARC §3; MEM:606-613 (`snmvp-docs/findings/phase1_results.md`).
- **A pin-trained VLA is HOSTAGE to the prior: served with a random c it scores 0.0 vs 0.8 for scratch on an in-source task — pass-through faithfully executes the bad instruction.** — MEM:1180-1196.
- **Linear state→c was the whole gate problem; NONLINEARITY (not vision) fixes it: MLP(state,lang) 0.66 vs linear 0.46; subspace-R² -0.20→+0.65.** — `rung3/train_c_predictor*.py`, `eval_offline_lang.py`; MEM:1466-1490.
- **~0.66 is the feedforward ceiling on real gate data (NOT feature-limited): resnet18 (0.54), pi0 flow-embed (0.56), state-history+vel (0.65) all fail to beat single-frame MLP(state,lang); gap to oracle 0.97 = irreducible teleop jitter.** — `rung3/train_c_predictor_{v2,flowemb,temporal}.py`; MEM:1474-1485.
- **(state+language) handles BOTH regimes; language is needed even on "state-driven" object tasks (state alone −0.41): proprio can't identify WHICH object.** — `rung3/eval_offline_lang.py`; MEM:1428-1445.
- **Gate pin c is 99% STATE-driven (lang ~1%): RMS state-driven 11.4 vs lang-driven 4.3 — the pin is strong state feedback, not language-only.** — `rung3/probe_pin_state.py`; MEM:1861-1873.
- **Input-noise AUGMENTATION on state→c is THE causal closed-loop stability lever (robustness > on-dist accuracy): AUG=0.15 eliminates runaway (endx-std 1872→1.5); AUG=0.05 → catastrophic 1e7 divergence.** — `rung3/make_hist_prior.py`; MEM:1875-1900.
- **PROGRESS-conditioning (c=MLP([state,onehot,t/T])) unlocks leading-to-end: in-zone 0%→60%; plain monotone CLOCK beats "smarter"/history-derived progress (60% vs 10% vs 0%).** — `rung3/make_progress_prior.py`; MEM:1974-2007.

## Steerability & interpretability
- **The source-noise pin is the most precise, disentangled, EXTRAPOLATING steering channel: slope 1.00 / phase err 1.1° / cross-leak 0.018; conditioning & CSFM steer direction but SATURATE in magnitude (~60% of a 2× cmd) and leak more.** — `steer_probe.py`; MEM:261-282.
- **Measure steering by DIRECT cmd→produced sweeps, not finite-diff Jacobians (the earlier "pin under-responds slope 0.48" was an artifact).** — MEM:261-282.
- **At 6-DOF one interpretable coordinate direction (`d_side`) steers the coupled position-detour + orientation-bank as a unit through a sign flip; realized-vs-commanded slope 1.001; pinned coords corr ~0.97 detour side, ~1.0 bank angle.** — ARC §2.7; `rung3/steer6d.py`; MEM:615-634.
- **Sweeping ONE pin coord fails (redundant encoding → pin fights itself); must move along the empirical side-direction. Steerability/interpretability are UNCONDITIONAL (don't need the bottleneck), unlike the success lift.** — ARC §3; MEM:618-629.

## Cross-embodiment transfer (robosuite, offline geometric)
- **Same-task cross-ARM transfer WORKS: freeze GLAP subspace on {Panda,IIWA,UR5e} + relearn only executor on Jaco → 0.225→0.75 = Jaco's own ceiling; GLAP 0.75 > Fourier 0.383 > random 0.30.** — ARC §2.8; `rung3/transfer6d.py`; MEM:636-660.

- **Embodiment-invariance GENERALIZES across tasks/directions: held-out-arm rel err == in-sample within noise; bank↔vertical reconstruct each other (direction lives in the coordinate, not the basis).** — `rung3/taskembod_study.py`; MEM:881-901.
- **Variable-action-dimension transfer FAILS (drone north star): frozen 3-ch position subspace LOWERS success across a 3↔6-ch controller change (UR5e 0.808→0.625); oracle also ≤ scratch.** — ARC §5.2; `rung3/vardof_transfer.py`; MEM:692-714.
- **Mechanism: transfer works when the STORED action is genuinely shared task-space (same OSC_POSE controller); pinning the ACHIEVED trajectory is partly REALIZATION, so a controller/DOF change breaks it.** — MEM:704-710.
- **Structural reframe: the bottleneck in every positive result is the 6-ch pos-ori COUPLING; position-only tasks are unbottlenecked at convergence → can't have both the bottleneck AND an action-dim change in this task family. Verify bottleneck AT CONVERGENCE (a 4000-iter ckpt looked bottlenecked; 9000-iter reached ceiling).** — `rung3/vardof_complement.py`; MEM:846-879.
- **Strategy is reliable for exactly two situations (cross-arm same-task; sim→real same body), both holding the action interface fixed; NOT a general method.** — ARC §5.4; MEM:809-830.

## Realization / complement, deconvolution & Laplace
- **NAIVE planned-path pin FAILS (below scratch): pass-through clamps the achieved coord to the planned value but real tracking deviates; the plan→achieved map must be MODELED (deconvolution), not clamped.** — `rung3/complement_study.py`; MEM:1151-1178.
- **Achieved-pin coordinate is dynamics-ROBUST whenever the task is feasible (ringing is high-freq, doesn't touch the low-freq grid-Lap coord); breaking it requires a genuine controller/DOF change.** — MEM:1163-1178.
- **System-ID + deconvolution (FIR plan→achieved) reaches ~1.0 sim→real from 5 demos > additive pin (0.69) BUT not a fair comparison (lenient metric, starts from a valid planned reference); does NOT carry to a different arm (Jaco 0.14).** — `rung3/simreal_deconv.py`, `deconv_eval.py`; MEM:759-773, 809-824.
- **Laplace/z-transform helps only the REALIZATION (achieved(z)=planned(z)·H(z)), not the smooth instruction subspace; idealized LTI toy: transfer-function factoring ~4× better than additive at the largest pole gap.** — `rung3/laplace_toy.py`; MEM:716-736.
