## The idea

Flow-matching VLA action heads start from Gaussian source noise. We move the
control signal INTO that noise: pin a movement invariant (chunk-level
quantity, e.g. summed action deltas = "where does this motion end up") into
the source so the interpolant carries it at every noise level and the
training loss itself penalizes deviation. Control lives in the regression
target instead of in a conditioning input the network is free to ignore.
Adapted from an ICLR'26 paper on subspace phase-invariant sources for image
re-rendering; π0 on LIBERO is the testbed.

## The story so far — six findings, in the order they forced themselves on us

**1. The channel binds far harder than conditioning.** Toy first (26x
tighter execution of contradictory commands than an identical-information
conditioning branch), then at full scale: a π0 fine-tuned with the pin
executes contradictory commands at err 7.1 vs 111 for chance (16x), oracle
commands at 2.3% of dataset scale, follow rate 16/16 at every checkpoint,
across seeds (s43 tracking s42's curve exactly). Steering is also
metrically linear in the real sim loop: commanded invariant -> realized EE
displacement fits an affine map at R^2 0.87-0.97 (~3-4.7 mm per unit), so
"move 20 cm left" is a computable command.

**2. The pin must be always-on; there is no free dual-mode model.**
CFG-style pin dropout kills the channel (toy: 90x worse err-to-command; a
CFG-faithful presence-flag input does not rescue it). Once the model must
learn the obs->action mapping for unpinned samples, that strategy covers
all samples and nothing maintains the pin-read. Consequence: an always-
pinned policy needs a command source at every inference call.

**3. Structure can be DISCOVERED, and discovered beats hand-defined.**
(toy_frame, all 4 gates passed.) A coherence criterion over multiple demos
of the same scene — phase agreement of learned projections, mod-pi variant
for bimodal styles, energy floor against zero-energy "dust" — recovers the
planted frame, and a scene-prior supplies the pin with no oracle at eval:
**62.3% held-out success vs 45.1% (no pin) vs 44.1% (random-frame control,
same architecture)** — +17 pts, all seeds, 4.3x the pre-registered bar.
The hand-defined displacement pin scored 43.8% on the same task. Two
riders: (a) phase-only pins FAILED first (G3 below baseline) — in control,
safety-critical structure is partly metric (clearance amplitude), so pins
must carry complex coefficients (phase AND magnitude), not phase alone —
the paper's phase/appearance split is image-specific; (b) mod-pi line pins
+ orientation-from-noise preserve style diversity by construction (side
split 0.487 under full pinning).

**4. Geometric command oracles fail at contact.** Four oracle variants for
sim eval all scored 0/20 despite perfect far-field steering (EE homes
0.33->0.08 m on command): at grasp states the demos are stereotyped, the
correct "invariant" is the maneuver itself (hold-close-lift), and the pin
faithfully executes the WRONG geometric command through it (one variant got
a real vision-driven grasp and 3.2 cm lift before the still-commanding-
descend pin killed it). Chunk displacement is the wrong command language
for contact phases. Pin authority is state-dependent: strong in free
space, weaker where demos are stereotyped.

**5. A learned prior fixes what oracles couldn't** (D6, the toy_frame
configuration at LIBERO scale). A small CNN+MLP p(invariant | base image,
wrist image, raw state), trained on demo frames in an afternoon, predicts
the demo invariant at ~20-30% relative error — and because it predicts
demo-LIKE invariants at contact states, the self-contained pipeline
(checkpoint + prior + calibrated noise, zero sim-state reads) completes
pick-and-place at **46% canonical / 51% held-out**, up from 0%.

**6. The result is a coupling spectrum, measured in all three cells**
(B completed 2026-07-06 ~13:00 UTC). Same information, same prior, same
data — the only variable is where the signal enters:

| arm | adherence under contradiction | follow rate | self-contained success (canonical / held-out) |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (no channel) | — | — | 88-91% / 88% |
| B (conditioning token) | 82.7 (~chance); NEGATED cmds land WORSE than chance (138.8) — the model follows the scene instead | 0.625 | 76% / 73% |
| C (noise pin) | **7.1-8.2** (16x tighter than chance; oracle cmds 2.8-3.3) | 1.000 | 46-51% / 51-52% |

Both non-A arms are captured by their channels (stock eval: B 0-4%, C 0% —
"conditioning is ignorable" is false at scale). But the coupling STRENGTH
differs qualitatively: conditioning treats commands as a soft hint vision
can override — it recovers most of A's success from a noisy prior but
cannot be steered against the scene AT ALL; the pin binds hard — fully
steerable, and it faithfully executes the command source's ~25% errors as a
~40-pt success tax. Neither placement protocol shows a generalization gap
for any arm (the plan's held-out-success framing of H1 is empirically dead
at this scale; the benchmark doesn't discriminate placements). The
obedience-success frontier IS the Phase 1 result.

## Open questions for discussion

1. **Framing the headline.** "Obedience-precision tradeoff" with C
   steerable-at-50% vs A unsteerable-at-88% — is that the paper's story, or
   do we attack the tax first (prior-v2: temporal context, bigger net,
   confidence-gated alpha, per-replan residual prediction) and try to close
   the gap before writing?
2. **B answered both questions** (finding 6): C wins adherence by 11x-to-
   unbounded (B follows negated commands WORSE than chance); B wins
   self-contained success by ~25 pts. The channel claim is complete; the
   framing question (Q1) is now the only open one. A third-way question
   for discussion: is there a principled interpolation (e.g. pin a SUBSET
   of dims, condition the rest; or soft-pin alpha<1) that traces the
   frontier between B's and C's operating points?
3. **Phase 2 design** now has three hard constraints from the data:
   always-on codes + prior at inference (finding 2), codes must carry
   metric content (finding 3a), and codes should probably be time-localized
   so contact phases carry their own code (finding 4). Coherence-discovery
   stays live (finding 3); VQ-on-residual remains the layered option.
4. **Is "no placement generalization gap anywhere" a result?** It
   contradicts the plan's assumption that LIBERO-Spatial held-out
   placements would be the H1 discriminator. Worth reporting as a negative
   finding about the benchmark, or just an implementation footnote?
